# Judge Demo

1. Put four Android phones in one room.
2. Disable Wi-Fi and mobile data on every phone.
3. Enable Mesh Mode.
4. Confirm actual BLE peers appear.
5. Move A and D out of direct range where practical.
6. Send A→D and show the route/hop state.
7. Trigger SOS and show five pulses, vibration, sender and offline coordinates.
8. Send a short voice recording and show local reassembly.
9. Repeat a suspicious message and show Safety Review.
10. Break one link, send another message, restore the link and show queued delivery.
11. Open Event Log to show local receive/relay/ACK history.
